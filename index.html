Payers: Star Ratings came out Oct 8

2027 Star Ratings put a revenue figure on adherence and readmission measures that AI care managers can work on directly.

01
Alignment HealthcareCare OrchestrationHealth plan · Medicare Advantage

Its largest contract, H3815 (about 75% of members), falls from 4.0 to 3.5 Stars for 2027. The company's 8-K cites triple-weighted Health Outcomes Survey and Part D measures and commits to expanding care gap closure programs.

First move: medication adherence and gap closure campaigns aimed at the triple-weighted Part D measures, pitched to the quality team that just made a public commitment.

Alignment 8-K, Oct 2026 · CMS 2027 measure weights
02
Aetna (CVS Health)Care OrchestrationHealth plan · Medicare Advantage

Share of MA members in 4+ star plans fell to about 69% for 2027 from 81%. Three of its four largest contracts declined and two fell below 4 stars.

First move: adherence, screening and readmission campaigns. Plan All-Cause Readmissions is also triple-weighted, so one program can serve both the Stars and the readmission story.

Fierce Healthcare, Oct 9, 2026
03
MassHealth ACOs and MCOCare OrchestrationValue-based care · Massachusetts

The Community Partners care management programs ended June 30, 2026. From July 1, ACOs no longer have to contract with them, and they carry a new care management enrollment target of about 3.5 to 4% of member months.

First move: offer AI care managers as the replacement capacity for ACOs that now have to run care management themselves.

MassHealth Managed Care Entity Bulletin 144

How this was built

An AI research agent ran these four sweeps, opened every source and kept only signals with a dated primary or trade source. I set the method, supplied the system mapping, chose what counted as a buying signal and wrote the first moves. Steps 1 and 3 can rerun every October.

  1. Readmissions rolled up to accounts. Downloaded CMS's FY2027 HRRP supplemental file and joined it by certification number to my own hospital-to-health-system mapping, then ranked systems by penalized volume and average penalty. Press coverage named no hospitals, and CMS names no owners.
  2. Procurement sweeps. Searched federal aggregators and state bid boards for nurse advice line, nurse triage and care management solicitations, and tracked Medicaid re-procurements through a weekly RFP calendar, confirming each on the agency page.
  3. Payer pressure. After the Oct 8 Star Ratings release, read trade analyses and company 8-Ks, then tied each drop to CMS's 2027 measure weights to find triple-weighted adherence and readmission measures.
  4. Policy and oversight feeds. Read state Medicaid bulletins and inspector general reports for care management and call center changes with a dated source.

Caveats

  • The system mapping dates from May 2026; check recent acquisitions and divestitures before outreach. 419 program hospitals did not match and are left out of the ranking.
  • The DHA award status is not public, so treat it as an awards watch, not an open bid.
  • IHS lines may be restricted to Indian small businesses, so they can only be reached through a partner.
  • Backup payer targets with 2027 Star declines: UnitedHealthcare, HCSC and Centene.

About this brief

Rolled up from hospitals to the health systems that buy, the new readmission penalties point to a few systems where nearly every hospital is penalized and one decision covers them all. Eight more signals from health plans and public buyers follow, each with a source and a first move.

CMS's FY2027 readmission penalties took effect Oct 1, 2026, and CMS publishes them hospital by hospital. Rolled up to the systems that buy, a few stand out: nearly every hospital penalized, a high average cut, and one decision that covers them all.
